## Content
Structured comparative review of four empirical studies (Aher et al. 2023; Santurkar et al. 2023; Vykopal et al. 2024; Gaeta et al. 2025) **plus an own experiment** (Section 5): a replication of the wisdom-of-crowds Turing Experiment with Qwen2.5-0.5B base vs. instruct. Result: instruction tuning alone did not produce the hyper-accuracy distortion (exact answers 4.0 % base vs. 1.4 % / 1.0 % instruct; no question with IQR = 0).
